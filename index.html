Selected Work & Research

Evaluation Standards for Agentic Educational AI

Systematic review, in progress (2026)

A PRISMA 2020 systematic review of how empirical studies actually evaluate LLM-based agentic and multi-agent educational systems — tutors, feedback generators, teachable agents, teacher-support tools. I code the field's evaluation designs, outcome constructs, methodological rigor, theoretical grounding, and reproducibility against a multi-dimensional rubric, and synthesize the result into a proposed reporting standard (AEER — Agentic Educational AI Evaluation & Reporting) aimed at raising the evidentiary bar for the field. The framing question is deliberately not what can these systems do, but does the evidence that they help people learn actually hold up — which is where most current work is thin.

In progress — preprint/OSF link to follow.

Financial Time-Series Signal Pipeline

Applied ML, ongoing (private)

A modular, production-style machine-learning pipeline for directional prediction on financial time-series data — built as clean, importable Python components spanning ingestion, feature engineering, gradient-boosted modeling, and a walk-forward backtesting engine, with rigorous out-of-sample, regime-aware evaluation across more than a decade of data. The emphasis is on honest validation: characterizing not just whether a signal works, but the specific conditions under which it does and doesn't. Implementation details held private pending further development.
